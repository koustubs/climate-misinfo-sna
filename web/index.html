        How it works

        Methods, data and limitations

        Data

        DIGSUM cop26-27-tweets network subset (Zenodo, March 2026): 674,485 anonymised tweet records from the COP26 (2021) and COP27 (2022) climate summits, with retweet/quote/reply targets, Infomap community IDs and a misinformation probability per post from the authors' classifier (flag = probability ≥ 0.5). No tweet text is included.

        1 · Graph construction

        Directed graph per conference. Edge author → retweeter when the retweeted author also posted in the same subset. Repeated retweets collapse to one edge; self-retweets are removed.

        2 · Communities

        Louvain maximises modularity Q = (1/2m) Σij [Aij − kikj/2m] δ(ci, cj) on the undirected, retweet-weighted graph. We compare it with the dataset's Infomap partition using NMI and ARI.

        3 · Centrality

        Degree: number of distinct retweeters. Betweenness CB(v) = Σ σst(v)/σst, estimated with Brandes' algorithm from 128 sampled sources. PageRank on the reversed graph (retweeter → author), damping 0.85. k-core number on the undirected graph.

        4 · Spreader detection

        Account label: spreader if ≥ 20% of its posts are flagged (accounts with ≥ 3 posts). 16 features in three groups - behaviour, network structure and homophily (share of neighbours who are known spreaders, computed out-of-fold from training labels only). Stratified 70/30 split; metrics on the held-out 30%.

        5 · Fact-check experiment

        Independent Cascade in live-edge form: each edge is live with probability p; the claim reaches everything reachable from the seeds. Fact-checked accounts receive but never forward. Reduction = 1 − mean reach / mean baseline reach. 95% intervals from 2,000 paired bootstrap resamples.

        Limitations

        • About 21% of retweets point to accounts outside the released subset, so the graphs are partial.
        • Labels are the dataset classifier's outputs; without tweet text they cannot be audited here.
        • Perfect stopping after a fact check is an idealised assumption - real corrections are weaker.
        • Posts are from 2021-22; the dataset was released in 2026.
